# Golden Angle privacy policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

Golden Angle is a game for Wear OS watches made by Phil Sherwin-Nicholls.

**Golden Angle does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.**

- The app has no internet permission and never connects to a network.
- It has no adverts, analytics, crash reporting or third-party code that collects data.
- It needs no account and no sign-in.
- The only things it stores are your best score, the furthest bloom you reached, your crown-speed setting, and whether you've seen the first-game hints. They're kept privately on your watch, in the app's own storage, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The app uses the watch's vibration motor for game feedback, and keeps the screen awake only while a round is being played.

Because no data is collected, there's nothing to access, correct or delete on request. If you have a question, contact: psherwinnicholls@gmail.com
